# Shivansh Chauhan

Data and BI analyst with over six years in reporting and analytics, based in London. I care most about whether the numbers coming out of a system can be trusted, so every figure in my projects is reconciled back to source.

At HCLTech I spent two years as a Senior Analyst building Power BI reporting for Emirates Global Aluminium in Dubai, where I cut repeat SLA breaches 15% and reached 90%+ closure of corrective actions. At smallcase I lifted onboarding conversion 14% through funnel and cohort analysis. I have an MSc in Business Analytics from Aston University (Merit).

## Projects

**Northstar: Causal Demand, Promotion and Inventory Analytics**
Tests how much promotions really earn on a synthetic 20-store UK grocery chain. The standard lift measure overstated returns (+126.7% against a true +81.0%), and a difference-in-differences correction brought it to +96.4%. Includes a five-page Power BI report on a star schema with 45 DAX measures.
`Python` `DuckDB` `Power BI` `DAX` `Causal inference`

**UK Corporate Compliance Radar**
Ranks 10,000 Companies House records into a risk watchlist, using a bronze-silver-gold PostgreSQL warehouse, a star schema and a Power BI drill-through report. Directors are pseudonymised under UK GDPR, and an LLM screening layer was hand-verified against source accounts.
`PostgreSQL` `SQL` `Power BI` `DAX` `Python`

**Atlas: Validated Analytics Pipeline**
A pipeline that blocks any result it cannot independently re-derive, with 18 specialist agents and 380 automated tests. Its validator vetoed a model at chance-level accuracy (AUC 0.4954) before release.
`Python` `DuckDB` `LLM tool-calling` `Automated testing`
